Funkadelic Astronaut

Funkadelic Astronaut is a New Jersey funktronica trio featuring Ryan Gavin (keyboards), Kevin O’Neill (drums), and Sam Luba (bass and vocals). Founded in 2012 by high school friends Ryan and Kevin, the band solidified its lineup when Sam joined in 2017 and has performed across the Northeast.
